A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before “I do”.

How can I receive a wedding package proposal?+

Choose your preferred package and share a few details about your plans. Our Wedding Specialist will confirm pricing, menus, minimum guest numbers, and benefits in a proposal tailored to your celebration.

Can I visit the venue before making a decision?+

Use the inquiry form to request a venue visit with our Wedding Specialist. Visits are arranged around team availability and the venue’s event schedule.

Can I personalise my wedding celebration?+

Share your vision and preferences with the Westin team. Layouts, menus, decor, vendors, and additional services can be discussed and confirmed in your proposal.
